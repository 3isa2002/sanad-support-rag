# Payments & Wallet

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza), Souqna wallet, mobile wallets and
cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 EGP.

## Installments
Orders above 1,500 EGP can be paid in installments through partner banks and installment
providers. Available plans are shown at checkout.

## Souqna wallet
The wallet holds refunds and credits. Wallet balance never expires and can be used on any
order, but it cannot be withdrawn as cash except for cash-on-delivery refunds.

## Payment failed but money was deducted
If a card payment failed but the amount was deducted, it is usually reversed automatically by
your bank within 7 business days. If it is not, contact support with the order number.
