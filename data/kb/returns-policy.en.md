# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 14 days of delivery. Electronics have a 7-day return window.
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories and the invoice.

## Non-returnable items
Underwear, swimwear, cosmetics that have been opened, perishable food, gift cards and
digital products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or wrong.

## How to start a return
Open "My Orders", choose the order, tap "Return item" and pick a reason. A courier will
collect the item within 3 business days. Keep the item packed and ready for pickup.

## Refund timing
After the item passes inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method.
Card refunds take 5–10 business days to appear; wallet refunds are instant;
cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to your Souqna wallet or by bank transfer within 7 business days.

## Damaged or wrong items
If you received a damaged, defective or wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery with
photos. Return shipping is free in this case and you can choose a replacement or a full refund.
