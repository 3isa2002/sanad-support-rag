# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery times
Orders inside Cairo and Giza are delivered in 1–3 business days. Other governorates take
3–6 business days. Items sold by third-party sellers may need 1–2 extra days to reach our warehouse.

## Shipping fees
Shipping is free for orders above 500 EGP from items marked "Fulfilled by Souqna".
Otherwise the fee is 35 EGP inside Cairo and Giza and 55 EGP for other governorates.

## Tracking an order
Once your order ships you get an SMS and email with a tracking number. You can also see the
live status in "My Orders" or ask the assistant with your order number (for example ORD-10023).

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the address while the order status is "Processing". After it ships, the address
cannot be changed, but you can ask the courier to reschedule the delivery.

## Failed delivery
The courier tries to deliver twice. If both attempts fail, the order returns to the seller and
prepaid amounts are refunded to your wallet within 3 business days.
