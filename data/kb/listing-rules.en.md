# Listing Rules for Sellers

## Listing review
Every new or edited listing is reviewed before it goes live. Reviews usually finish within
24 hours. While under review the listing is not visible to buyers.

## Why a listing gets rejected
Common reasons: blurry or watermarked photos, missing price, wrong category, contact details
(phone numbers or links) in the title or description, or prohibited items. The rejection
reason is shown on the listing page; fix it and resubmit.

## Prohibited items
Weapons, drugs and medicines, counterfeit or replica goods, live animals, tobacco, and
stolen goods are not allowed. Repeated violations lead to account suspension.

## Listing expiry
Listings stay active for 60 days. You will be notified 3 days before expiry and can renew
the listing for free from "My Listings".

## Photo requirements
Upload at least 2 clear photos, minimum 800×800 pixels, on a plain background, with no text,
logos or watermarks.
