# Account & Safety

## Resetting your password
Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen and enter your phone number or email. You will get
a one-time code valid for 10 minutes.

## Staying safe from scams
Souqna staff will never ask for your password, card number or one-time code. Always pay
inside the app; never transfer money to a seller outside Souqna.

## Deleting your account
Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent after 30 days; you can log in
during that period to cancel it. Accounts with open orders cannot be deleted.
